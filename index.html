Connect your database

Connect Upstash Redis through Vercel so your published changes are saved for everyone.

Connect Upstash on Vercel ↗
  1. In Vercel, add Upstash and connect a Redis database to this project and environment.
  2. In project settings, add ADMIN_PASSWORD and a separate SESSION_SECRET of at least 32 characters.
  3. Redeploy your project, then return here and check setup.

Vercel may ask you to sign in, approve the integration, or choose a plan. Database credentials stay in Vercel.
